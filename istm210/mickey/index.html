Mouse House Detailing is a student-run service for customers who want a clean vehicle without losing an afternoon. Appointments fit around class schedules, and every price is stated before work begins. Customers approve the service level before the appointment. The business focuses on the College Station area so travel time stays predictable. Students manage scheduling, supplies, and customer follow-up themselves. A written checklist keeps every service consistent. Monthly reviews compare customer feedback with supply costs. The team uses those results to improve the next month rather than guessing.
