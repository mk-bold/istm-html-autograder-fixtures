Correct AI Usage

Students can use AI to explain a difficult concept and suggest practice questions. They should compare its claims with course materials and reliable sources. They should disclose help when a course requires it. They must keep responsibility for every submitted choice.

The AI agreed and added that students should avoid uploading private information or unpublished work.
